# Data agent guide

Keep claim entries specific, source-linked, and assumption-aware. Do not copy
numbers from a rendered PDF, dashboard, or terminal into the ledger. If a
claim is generated, identify its producer, the result artifact that records its
seed/configuration, and the evidence class that bounds the claim.

`claim_ledger.yaml`, benchmark manifests, and frozen research-lane YAML files
are source contracts. A change to any of them invalidates source-bound outputs
and requires the documented generation/hydration/render sequence; it is not a
reason to patch a generated receipt. Keep literature keys separate from
empirical artifacts, preserve failed campaign receipts, and never recast a
stress or calibration diagnostic as a formal guarantee.
